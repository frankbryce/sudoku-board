Privacy Policy

DoDad Puzzles LLC • Sewickley, PA 15143
Last updated: November 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DoDad Puzzles LLC (“DoDad Puzzles™,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you visit dodadpuzzles.com.

1. Information We Collect on This Site

dodadpuzzles.com is a static website. We do not use advertising trackers, behavioral analytics scripts, or first-party tracking cookies on this site, and we do not sell your personal information.

  • Mailing List Signups: If you choose to join our mailing list, we collect your email address and any optional topic preferences you select (such as shipped kits or new free puzzles). Signups are submitted via Google Apps Script and stored in a private Google Sheet owned by DoDad Puzzles LLC. We use this list strictly to send occasional DoDad Puzzles™ email updates (using BCC so your address stays private from other subscribers). You can unsubscribe at any time on our Unsubscribe page, by replying to any email, or by messaging us on social media.
  • Hosting & Infrastructure Logs: This site is hosted via GitHub Pages. When you load a page, standard web request data (such as IP address, browser type, and requested URL) may be processed by GitHub in accordance with the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Web Fonts: Typography is served via Google Fonts, which receives standard HTTP request headers when your browser loads font files.

2. Third-Party Platforms & Future Orders

Our site links to external services that operate under their own privacy policies:

  • GitHub: Free STL files, OpenSCAD source, and printable PDF workbooks are hosted at github.com/frankbryce/sudoku-board.
  • Substack & X: If you subscribe to updates on Substack (@dodadpuzzles) or message us on X (@dodadpuzzles), those platforms collect and manage your information under their respective privacy policies.
  • Shipped Physical Kits: When direct checkout for physical DoDad Puzzles™ sets launches, payment and shipping details will be processed by trusted third-party commerce and shipping providers strictly to fulfill your order and provide customer support.

3. Children’s Privacy

DoDad Puzzles™ designs hands-on puzzles for parents and kids to enjoy together at home, but this website is intended for parents, educators, and adult buyers. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

4. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as we add new features or launch direct online ordering. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach DoDad Puzzles LLC via @dodadpuzzles on X, substack.com/@dodadpuzzles, or by referencing:
